Thickness, one component, sizes

Minimum thickness

From 5 × 5 points on each face, 10% in from its edges, shoot a ray straight inward. Newton tracks it until it leaves the piece's cell; bisection finds the exit. The shortest ray is the thickness.

One component

True by construction: a piece is one box pushed through a continuous map. It can only fail by folding (det J < 0), which the Jacobian check catches.

Uniform sizes

Volume = ∫ det J over the piece's cell (6 × 6 × 6 midpoint rule). Report largest / smallest volume: pass ≤ 2, warn ≤ 3.

Joints exposed in cavities: not applicable yet, because this experiment has no joints.

Thickness details: faces that collapse to a point or a curve (the centre of the ball, the duck's tip) are skipped, because their normal is round-off and the ray grazes along a neighbouring cut. The 10% inset exists because material always tapers to nothing at a sharp edge, like the rim of a hemisphere. Limit in the app: a slider in percent of model size, default 3%, which is arbitrary until we have a printing size in mm.
